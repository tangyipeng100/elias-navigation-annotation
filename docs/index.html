01 / OVERVIEW

Separate the evidence before writing the instruction.

A multimodal model can describe a recorded trajectory fluently while misidentifying a region, omitting a turn, or confusing an object ahead with the stopping place. These errors weaken trajectory–instruction pairs.

ELIAS assigns distinct roles to ordered frames, terminal views, lateral observations, motion geometry, and scene priors. Open terminal observation precedes canonical retrieval; a separate consistency gate decides accept, review, or abstain.

02 / METHOD

Scene priors meet staged grounding.

The prior supplies canonical names and spatial context, not proof that a route enters a region. Open terminal observation remains prior-free.

SELECT A STAGE TO EXPLORE

01

Region inference

Route frames, motion geometry, and the scene vocabulary establish start, goal, and traversed regions. Visible does not mean entered; unknown remains valid when evidence is insufficient.

Input: route observations + motion geometry + scene prior.

“Evidence locked” means grounded structured evidence constrains subsequent generation and remains auditable—not an immutable final-sentence overwrite. The full pipeline uses five model calls and one local retrieval operation.

Paired comparison

Critical-error change: −70 percentage points on rendered routes, 95% interval [−90, −45]; −80 on full robot routes, [−100, −50]. Holm-adjusted exact McNemar p values are .0031 and .0391.

The acceptance gate is not a guarantee

The full pipeline accepts 14 of 30 complete candidates; 2 accepted outputs still have critical errors (14.3%). The gate prioritizes review rather than certifying correctness.

Evaluation protocol and limits

40 sample groups, 120 candidates. Method names, generation intermediates, and generator self-reviews are hidden from the evaluator. Intervals use 2,000 paired bootstrap replicates; six predefined comparisons use Holm correction. One building system and one model evaluator limit generalization; component effects remain preliminary. No human gold set, inter-rater agreement, downstream navigation evaluation, or measured labor saving is established. Region-only robot errors fall from 7/10 to 4/10, adjusted p=.7500—not a significant improvement.
